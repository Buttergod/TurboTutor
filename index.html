Quick answers

Before the start

6 answers

What subjects can my student choose?

Choose Science, English, or Math for any grade from 1 through 8. You can add more than one course to a single request.

What does each plan include?

The weekly plan includes one class per week, the monthly plan includes four, and the yearly plan includes 48 across 12 months. Choose a 30-, 45-, or 60-minute class for every plan. Yearly enrollment is paid upfront and includes 10% savings versus 12 monthly payments.

How is pricing calculated?

Rates vary by grade and class length. Monthly plans include four weekly classes. Yearly plans include 48 classes and are priced 10% below 12 monthly payments. The tutor confirms schedule availability before enrollment.

How do I request enrollment?

Add courses, enter your contact details and payment preference, then choose an email or text draft. Your email or messaging app opens with the request filled in. Review it and press Send to contact the tutor. No charge is made by this site.

Can I pay by cash or Zelle?

Select cash or Zelle in the request form. No payment is collected on this website; confirm payment instructions directly with the tutor.

How does the welcome wheel work?

The optional parent/guardian spin is free and limited to one spin per browser every 30 days. The odds are 69% for 5% off the first monthly payment, 30% for $10 off the first invoice, and 1% for a 15-minute Zoom introduction. Offers expire 30 days after reveal and cannot be combined.
