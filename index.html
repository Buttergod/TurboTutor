Quick answers

Before the start

What subjects can my student choose?

Choose Science, English, or Math for any grade from 1 through 8. You can add more than one course to a single request.

What does each class type include?

Choose a single class, monthly class, or yearly class. The tutor will confirm the session schedule, exact class details, and price with you before you agree to enroll.

How do I request enrollment?

Add courses, enter your contact details and payment preference, then choose an email or text draft. Your email or messaging app opens with the request filled in. Review it and press Send to contact the tutor.

Can I pay by cash or Zelle?

Select cash or Zelle in the request form. No payment is collected on this website; confirm payment instructions directly with the tutor.
