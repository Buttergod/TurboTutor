Quick answers

Before the start

What subjects can my student choose?

Choose Science, English, or Math for any grade from 1 through 8. You can add more than one course to a single request.

What does each class type include?

A single class is one 45-minute session. The monthly plan includes four 45-minute sessions, and the yearly plan includes 48. Confirm the schedule and any specific learning needs with the tutor before enrolling.

Are the listed prices final?

The displayed rates are suggested launch prices because final pricing was not provided. Update them to your actual rates before publishing. You can confirm the total and schedule with the tutor before agreeing to enroll.

How do I request enrollment?

Add courses, enter your contact details and payment preference, then choose an email or text draft. Your email or messaging app opens with the request filled in. Review it and press Send to contact the tutor. No charge is made by this site.

Can I pay by cash or Zelle?

Select cash or Zelle in the request form. No payment is collected on this website; confirm payment instructions directly with the tutor.
